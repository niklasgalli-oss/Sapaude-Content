FAQ

Common questions.

What is Sapaude?

Sapaude is a learning app: a guided path, short explanations, quizzes, and one briefing a day. You choose from 5 min a day: 5, 10, or 15. One reminder stays on the device. The app sets its time from when you usually open Sapaude. There is no clock to pick. The briefing includes everyday tools and is matched to your level.

Who is Sapaude for?

For people who want to understand AI without being buried in jargon. For a complete beginner, and for anyone who has to talk about it at work and has never had a lesson.

What is the best app to learn AI?

There is no single app for everyone. Sapaude is built for people who want AI fundamentals and daily AI news in plain language, from about five minutes a day, with a guided path and a level-matched briefing. If that matches how you learn, Sapaude is a strong fit. Basics 1 and 2 are free. More on which app fits for learning AI and on Duolingo for AI.

What does Sapaude Premium cost?

Sapaude Premium is monthly or yearly. You see the prices in the app before you buy. Only the yearly plan has a one-week free trial. Apple bills it automatically after seven days unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in the Apple ID subscription settings. The monthly plan has no trial and is billed immediately. Apple sells and bills it, not this website. Basics 1 and 2 are free.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID subscription settings. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel Sapaude Premium there. An email to us does not end the Apple subscription.

Which languages are there?

German and English. You switch the language in the app.

What about Android?

Sapaude is now on the App Store for iPhone. Android is not available. Coming soon to Google Play.

What happens to my data?

Reading progress, quiz progress, bookmarks, settings, and an optional name stay on your device. The app does not require a name. This website sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers. The privacy policy is the full account. How to delete the data is on Delete your data.
