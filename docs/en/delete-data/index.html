Delete your data

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page applies to the Sapaude learning app on iOS and Android and to this website. Sapaude has no account and no login. There is no username and no password, and you do not create an account in the app. The privacy policy describes the same processing. The controller is the provider named in the legal notice (Impressum). The name and the postal address are stated there and are not repeated here.

How to delete your data

  1. Delete data on this device by deleting the Sapaude app or by removing the app data in the operating system. That covers reading progress, quiz progress, bookmarks, personalization, an optional name, settings, and the cache. The controller does not keep a copy on a server.
  2. Cancel a Sapaude Pro subscription in the Apple ID subscription settings or in Google Play. An email to the controller does not cancel the subscription and does not refund a payment.
  3. Delete the anonymous app user ID and the purchase data at RevenueCat by writing to support@sapaude.app. The controller answers the request within 30 days and instructs RevenueCat to delete those data.
  4. What is kept is listed under “What is kept”: the email itself for as long as handling it and any statutory period require, purchase records at Apple or Google, and, at RevenueCat, backup copies and data required for legal duties, resolving disputes, or enforcing agreements.

Data on the device

The app stores reading progress, quiz progress, bookmarks, personalization, settings, and a cache locally on your device, using AsyncStorage. You may enter a name in settings. The name is optional. The app does not require one. The name stays only on the device. That data is not sent to the controller. The controller does not keep a copy and cannot delete it from a server or hand it over from one.

On iPhone, open Settings, tap General, then iPhone Storage, then Sapaude, and choose Delete App. Or press and hold the app icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Offload App and Remove from Home Screen leave the data on the device. Choose Delete App.

On Android, open Settings, tap Apps, then Sapaude, then Storage, and choose Clear data or Clear storage. The label depends on the Android version. Uninstalling the app removes the same data.

That deletes reading progress, quiz progress, bookmarks, personalization, an optional name, settings, and the cache from this device. Deletion applies to this device. An installation on another device keeps its own local data.

Subscriptions at Apple and Google

Sapaude Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. Apple sells, bills, cancels, and refunds it through In-App Purchase. Where the app is offered on Google Play, the same applies through Google. The contract and the payment are made with the store, not on this website and not with the controller. The controller does not receive your payment-card number.

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel Sapaude Pro there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period or the trial, or Apple renews it. In Google Play, open Google Play, tap Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Sapaude Pro there. The stores describe the steps in their own help.

Apple and Google keep purchase and account data as their own controllers. They decide how long they keep it. The controller cannot delete those records. The stores’ notices: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Data at RevenueCat

The app asks RevenueCat whether the trial or the subscription is unlocked. RevenueCat, Inc., United States, processes this data as a processor for the controller. The data are an anonymous identifier of app use, purchase receipts and transaction data, the status of the trial and the subscription, and device and app details such as device type and operating system. That includes the Apple receipt file, the Google purchase token, and the time the app was last seen. The controller does not store these data on a server of his own. Payment and the store account remain with Apple or Google.

Deleting the app does not delete these data at RevenueCat. For deletion, write to support@sapaude.app. Say that you want the RevenueCat data for Sapaude deleted. Name the store, the Apple App Store or Google Play, and, if you started a subscription or a trial, the approximate date. Do not send a payment-card number.

The controller answers the request within 30 days and instructs RevenueCat to delete the anonymous identifier and the purchase data. Support confirms receipt within 48 hours. The period of 30 days is the time for carrying out the deletion. The request does not cancel the subscription and does not refund a payment.

The anonymous identifier is created on the device. It is not tied to an email address, and the app does not display it. A store and an approximate purchase date can match a purchase at RevenueCat. If there was no purchase, deleting the app removes the data on the device. The controller cannot find an anonymous identifier that cannot be matched to the email from the email alone. The reply says so when that is the case.

What is kept

  • Data on the device: after you delete the app or the app data, the controller retains nothing, because the controller does not receive it.
  • Apple and Google: purchase and account data stay with those providers, under their own rules. The controller does not receive the card number and does not delete those records.
  • RevenueCat: after the instruction, RevenueCat deletes the anonymous identifier and the purchase data. Ordinary backup and archive copies can remain until those copies expire. Data required for legal duties, resolving disputes, or enforcing agreements can stay stored for that period. RevenueCat describes a period of six years after an account ends for people who have an account of their own with RevenueCat. The data described here are an anonymous identifier and the purchase data, not the sign-in details of such an account.
  • Email to support@sapaude.app, including this deletion request: the controller processes your email address, the message, and the reply in order to handle the request. Mail is received by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, as processor. Correspondence is kept for as long as handling the request and any commercial or tax retention period require, and is then deleted.
  • This website: its own code sets no cookies and uses no analytics tool. There is no account on the website. When you open a page, GitHub Pages receives the connection data the request requires, in particular the IP address. The controller does not keep a copy of that.
