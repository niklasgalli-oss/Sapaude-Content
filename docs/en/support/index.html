Support

Questions about the app, Sapaude Pro, privacy, or the Sapaude for Teams waitlist go to support@sapaude.app.

Support requests are answered within 48 hours. No telephone number is published.

Basics 1 and 2, including listening, are free. Listening to the briefing is part of Sapaude Pro. You see the prices in the app before you buy. Apple sells and bills the subscription, not this website.

The name and the postal address are in the Impressum and are not repeated here. The privacy policy describes what happens to a message sent to this address. How to remove data on the device, and how to ask for deletion of the data at RevenueCat, is on Delete your data.

Common questions

Where do I download Sapaude?

Sapaude is coming soon on iPhone. Android follows. Until a listing is public, there is no store link. The home page says “coming soon” until then.

What does the app cost?

Basics 1 and 2, including listening, are free. Listening to the briefing is part of Sapaude Pro. You see the prices in the app before you buy. Which plan has a trial is in the FAQ on the home page.

How do I delete data?

On the device, and at RevenueCat, the steps are on Delete your data.

Which languages are there?

German and English. You switch the language in the app. The same answer is in the FAQ.

Where is the briefing on the web?

The long articles and the short items are under Briefing. The home page shows three cards from the current day even without JavaScript.
