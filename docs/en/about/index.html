About Sapaude

Sapaude is a learning app for AI fundamentals and a daily briefing, in German and English, from about five minutes a day. Sapaude in this text is that app at sapaude.app. It is not a place name and not a different product on another domain.

The name comes from sapere aude, dare to know. The mascot is Sapi. The line on the home page is “Be brave. Get smart.”

What the app does

A guided path in three levels: Basics, Core, Depth. Short lessons, quizzes, and one briefing a day, matched to your level in the app. You choose 5, 10, or 15 minutes. Basics 1 and 2 are free, including listening. Listening to the briefing is part of Sapaude Pro. You see the prices in the app before you buy. Apple sells and bills the subscription, not this website.

The app does not require a name. Progress stays on the device. Sapaude is coming soon on iPhone. Android follows. Until a store listing is public, this site does not link to one.

What this website is

Static pages. No cookies, no third-party trackers. The long briefing texts are HTML under Briefing, with a date and a source. The same files the app loads are at pulse.json and briefing.json.

Two guides explain the shape without a ranking: which app fits for learning AI and Duolingo for AI.

Contact

Questions go to support@sapaude.app. Replies within 48 hours. The name and the postal address are only in the Impressum and are not repeated here. Sapaude for Teams is a waitlist, not a finished product.
